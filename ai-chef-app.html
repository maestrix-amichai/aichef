Creamy Spinach Chicken Pasta
⏱️ 30 min
👥 4 servings
📊 Medium

🥗 Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 bunches fresh spinach, roughly chopped
  • 12 oz pasta (penne or rigatoni works great)
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream (or use that milk with a bit of flour)
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, and Italian herbs to taste

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Get a large pot of salted water boiling for the pasta. Once it's rolling, add your pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente.
  2. While the pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season the chicken pieces with salt and pepper, then add them to the hot pan.
  3. Cook the chicken for about 6-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, add the minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the diced tomatoes and cook for 3-4 minutes.
  5. Add the spinach to the pan and stir until it wilts down, about 2 minutes.
  6. Pour in the cream, bring to a gentle simmer, then add the Parmesan cheese and stir until melted and smooth.
  7. Return the chicken to the pan, add the drained pasta, and toss everything together. Season with Italian herbs.
  8. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan on top and enjoy your restaurant-quality meal!
